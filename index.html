Why medCaliper

Better technology begins with better listening.

Brain tumour care brings together complex imaging, clinical expertise and deeply personal decisions. We believe AI should help those elements work better together.

Our multidisciplinary team combines medical imaging, artificial intelligence, clinical neurosurgery and healthcare innovation. We are developing medCaliper alongside the people closest to the challenge so that it can fit real needs-not imagined ones.

Common questions

Clear by design.

medCaliper is still in development. These are the principles guiding the product and the conversations around it.

Is medCaliper a replacement for clinical judgement?

No. medCaliper is being developed as decision support: technology that complements clinical expertise and helps make complex information more accessible.

Who is medCaliper for?

Our work is focused on supporting professionals involved in brain tumour care, while keeping patient and family needs central to the design process.

Can I use medCaliper today?

medCaliper is currently in development and is not presented on this website as a clinical product available for use. You can help shape the work through our feedback links.

How can I collaborate with the team?

Email info@medcaliper.co.uk to discuss research, clinical or innovation collaboration.
